# Code of Conduct

SKILL.md is a collaborative project. Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good faith while checking evidence, welcome correction, and focus criticism on ideas, instructions, implementations, and outcomes rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, malicious contributions, credential exposure, deliberate safety bypasses, and disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that create material safety, legal, privacy, or quality risks.
