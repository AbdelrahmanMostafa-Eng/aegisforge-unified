## Status definitions

**Verified** means the stated evidence directly checks the requirement in the relevant environment. **Partially verified** means only part of the requirement or one environment was checked. **Unverified** means no reliable evidence is available. **Blocked** means verification could not be performed because a dependency, permission, or environment was unavailable. **Not applicable** must include a reason.
